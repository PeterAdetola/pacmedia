## Brief

Shores Hotel had a logo, a loyal guest base, and a growing property on
Lekki-Epe Expressway — but no website, no direct booking channel, and no
way to manage guest communication without routing everything through a
third party.

---

Build a website that lets guests book directly, responds to enquiries
quickly, and looks like the property it represents.

---

Every booking that went through an OTA was a guest relationship Shores
didn't own. The site needed to bring that relationship in-house — and give
the front-desk team the tools to maintain it.

## Process

Rather than adapt a generic hospitality template, the site was built around
how Shores Hotel actually operates — three distinct offerings (rooms,
apartments, CitiB Lounge) under one roof, each needing its own content
surface. Sitemap and admin workflows were agreed before any visual design
started, so the UI was built around real operations rather than retrofitted
to them.

## Outcome

The team took over the admin interface without hand-holding. Guest
communication, which previously had no formal channel, now runs through a
built-in mailbox — composing, replying, and sending booking confirmations
without leaving the dashboard.
