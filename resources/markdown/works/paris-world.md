## Brief

The existing mark — an ornate Eiffel Tower illustration — couldn't hold up
across packaging, signage, or digital at the scale the business was moving into.

---

Create a logo and design system that speaks to our evolution — replacing a
dated mark that can't scale or fit where the business stands today.

---

A mark that had served the storefront for years was quietly becoming a liability.
The new direction had to earn its place at every scale — from a 14mm badge to a
6-metre fascia — without compromise.

## Process

The brief pointed toward a PW monogram, but nothing resolved until we looked
inside the W in "World" — the Eiffel Tower was already there. The letterform
and the icon became inseparable: one mark, two readings, zero compromise.

6-metre storefront fascia — without losing resolution, weight, or recognition.
